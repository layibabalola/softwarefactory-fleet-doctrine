## Improvements

- **The prepared sink is resumed without a health check (Row A).** The sink can now sit open and suspended for minutes while paused. `syncAndPlayNow` takes `Mode::Prepared` from `m_prepared && m_audioEngineRunning` alone (`AudioPlayback.cpp`, `chooseMode(...)`). If the device changed or errored while paused (unplugged headphones, a new default output), `resume()` does nothing and the session plays silent. **Fix:** pass `m_pAudioOutput->state() == QAudio::SuspendedState && error() == QAudio::NoError` into `chooseMode` as a pure input, and re-open otherwise. Pin it in `PlaybackAudioStartPolicy` tests. Optionally drop the prepared sink on `QMediaDevices::audioOutputsChanged` (Qt 6).
- **Pausing before the first present can play a short audible blip.** The sequence: Play → cold `prepare` (about 600 ms) → first present queues `syncAndPlay` → user pauses → `stop()` queues. The worker then runs `syncAndPlay` with lag > 42 ms, so `Reopen` starts at volume 1, and the `stop` lands right after. **Fix:** stamp `syncAndPlay` with a play-session generation (an atomic bumped on Play and Pause, like `m_prepareTicket`) and drop stale syncs on the worker.
- **Row B's reset clears the live registration cache, not only the temporaries.** `gpuAmazeDebayerResetR16TextureBackendResources()` → `reset_live_gl_resources` (`tools/gpu/backend/igpu_amaze_debayer_cuda.cu:167-172`) clears the live backend's single-slot input/output GL registration cache. The prewarm therefore cannot warm the CUDA-GL registration of the real `m_texture` / recon-source texture. It also evicts any registration a paused texture-route present made, so the first Play present still pays `cudaGraphicsGLRegisterImage`. A later paused present at another size (scale 1 paused vs the x2/x4 plan) also re-sizes the live buffers the prewarm sized. There is no dangling state; ensure-on-mismatch re-registers. Correct the "unregister the temporaries" comment in `GpuDisplayWindow.cpp` and the policy docstring, and let the on/off D4 numbers (Blocker 1) show what Row B actually saves.
- **Behaviour changes that should be disclosed (body and CHANGELOG):**
  - (a) Whenever a clip with audio is paused with audio on in drop-frame mode, the app now keeps the OS audio device open, silent and suspended. A scrub re-opens it on the audio thread for each settled frame (coalesced).
  - (b) Row B moves a 170–400 ms GUI-thread stall, with `g_activeMutex` held, to just after every clip load on the CUDA texture route, including clips that are never played. Consider deferring it to an idle point and skipping it during batch/export.
- **Soft: the wiring tests are source-text pins only.** Examples are character offsets and `finish.mid(pausedPrepare - 400, 400)`. Nothing exercises the AudioPlayback queue's behaviour: ordering, coalescing, stop-keeps-prepared, and the shutdown join. A small QtTest with a stub sink would cover what the venue legs found the hard way (r1 legs a702dab3 and d1426fe1).
