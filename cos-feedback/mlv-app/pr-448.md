## Prevention

- **Gap:** the r4 `DEFEATER_AXIS` covers four defeater kinds per shape (possessive, attributive, trailing host, venue+host). But the new rule that made them pass is a *closed follow set*, and the axis never checks the members of that set. Every follow word that can open a non-locus complement (`to <verb>`, `for`, `as`, `that`, `with`, `so`) is unprobed, and the host-marker list is only probed in the one `on this host` form. The differential corpus has the same blind spot because its templates reuse the axis wordings.
- **Proposed check:** for any closed word list a lint exemption depends on (follow set, routing prepositions, host markers), the pin must generate one defeater row per list member: `locally using <venue> <word> <non-locus complement>` must refuse unless the word is in a documented locus-preserving subset. It should also generate one host-reference row per preposition × determiner × noun combination. An exemption shape can't be admitted with a list member that has no row.
