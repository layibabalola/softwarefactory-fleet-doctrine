## Blockers

   - `Run playback locally without the venue chain.`

   Cause: rule (2) at `lane-local-playback-lint.ps1` (the `$localMarker` loop) exempts on `$c.Venue`, and `Venue` is a bare match of `$venueQualified` in the clause text. `without`, `not`, `instead of` and `rather than` don't open a new clause (the comma separator only fires before a launch verb), so the negated venue word sits in the same clause as `locally`. This trades one over-refusal (the Sol probe) for several under-refusals in a safety guard, and the PR body's "No existing catalogue row changed verdict" is true only because the catalogue has no such row.

   **Unblock:** make venue routing polarity-aware. One way: treat `instead of | rather than | , not | , never | nor | without | skip | bypass` as a contrast boundary, where the complement after it inherits a strong negation and the clause before it keeps only its own venue words. Then `Run playback locally rather than on Bachelor` is refused (clause 1 has no venue), while the existing pass row `Run playback on Bachelor rather than locally` still passes (the `locally` complement is negated). Add the five sentences above to `CLAUSE_MUST_REFUSE` and keep every current `CLAUSE_MUST_PASS` row green.
