## Blockers

- **The in-pass contact-sheet grab adds an extra present/swap inside the measured Play** (shared Iverson Blocker, Claude + Codex; CoS-verified at `65195778`). Nothing corrects for it in the perf evidence.
  - **Extra present:** `MainWindow::noteContactSheetPresentedFrame` (`platform/qt/MainWindow.cpp:26163-26215`, called at `:29341` right after `notePlaybackSmokePresentedFrame`) runs on a presented frame while Play is checked and calls `GpuDisplayWindow::grabPresentedFramebufferIfActive`. That calls `paintGL()` again, then `glContext->swapBuffers(win)` and `noteRealSwap()` (`platform/qt/GpuDisplayWindow.cpp:1160-1182`). Paint-per-submit is **on by default** (`paintPerSubmitEnabled()`, `GpuDisplayWindow.cpp:88-94`: on unless `MLVAPP_GPU_WINDOW_PAINT_PER_SUBMIT=0`). So the frame was already painted and swapped at submit (`:990-998`), and each grab is a second, same-serial present plus a GUI-thread readback and a vsync-bound swap. With paint-per-submit off, the grab presents the pending frame early instead, and the queued `update()` paint still swaps.
  - **Counted:** `noteRealSwap()` (`GpuDisplayWindow.cpp:201-206`) counts every swap while the measured swap-telemetry session is active. Its own comment says "a queued or screenshot-capture swap must not be attributed to a finished session", but in-pass grabs now land **inside** the active session. They inflate `playback_smoke.gpu_window_swaps` `swaps` / `swap_fps` / `max_gap_ms`. `new_frame_swaps` dedupes repeated serials, but its gaps still absorb the readback stall. PresentMon traces every present the process makes across the measured window; the job proves PresentMon covers the app's swap window (`playback-attr-3-cuda-job.ps1:3419-3480`). So the duplicates also show up in PresentMon present intervals, the temporal max gap and cadence. (`playback_smoke.gpu_summary` frame-path counts come from the presented-frame hook and are not inflated themselves.)
  - **Not corrected:** `fps_excluding_grabs` only fixes the app's own fps figure in `gui_smoke.contact_sheet`. There is no capture-swap marker or count, and the attr-3 job reads none of `grab_total_ms` / `grab_ms` / `fps_excluding_grabs` (no matches in the tip job).
  - **On by default:** the PR drops the forced `--contact-sheet-seek-mode` from the attr-3 `-ContactSheet` args (`playback-attr-3-cuda-job.ps1:2491-2503`), so every `-ContactSheet` attr-3 leg is now in-pass. Every dual-venue `legType: look` leg sets `ContactSheet=$true` (`tools/profiling/dual-venue/Invoke-VenueLeg.ps1:284`) on each backend, including CUDA (`docs/dual-venue-evidence.md:271`). The `-ContactSheet` parameter comment (`:225-227`) still promises the capture "never perturbs the leg's fps/swap-cadence numbers", which is now false.
  - **Fix (any one):** grab without a second swap (e.g. read the presented frame from the normal paint path, `m_captureReadbackRequested` on the next real paint); or mark and subtract capture swaps (`capture_swaps=N` in `gpu_window_swaps`, with the job reading it and PresentMon intervals excluded around grabs); or keep `--contact-sheet-seek-mode` as the attr-3 / perf-leg default until fixed. Add a behavioural test that an in-pass capture does not increase the measured swap/present count, and fix the `:225-227` comment.
