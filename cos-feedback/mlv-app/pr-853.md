## I1 idle check precedes potentially slow binding

Deploy-BachelorAgent.ps1:548–562 runs Get-Refusals, then Get-RestartBindingRefusal, then native /End without another idle check. Binding can spend up to 20 seconds on heartbeat read and 30 seconds on Task Scheduler query plus output waits. If a job or lease arrives during successful binding, it may already be observable before /End, yet the agent is stopped; the later check at 566 only observes the arrival after stop. This unnecessarily widens the explicitly disclosed admission race; no executed job loss is claimed, and a final recheck alone is not an admission barrier.

After successful binding, recheck idle immediately before /End and refuse if busy, leased or unreadable. Retain post-stop warning for genuinely subsequent arrivals. Regression: have a successful binding seam introduce an inbox job, running work or lease; assert zero /End and /Run calls, with clean and binding-failure controls. Existing arrival test uses the alternate restart-script seam and does not exercise the native binding interval.
