## Blockers
- **SHARED BLOCKER (new on `c9bb51b5`; Dot 6c52523d, CoS-confirmed in code): the self-heal venue leg can PASS without proving L1-L3.** In the generated job (`playback-attr-3-cuda-job.ps1`, the `$preLaunchBlock` at `:4499-4531`), a pre-launch that is `PRE_LAUNCH_COMMAND_UNDERIVABLE`, throws, or exits non-zero only writes `pre-launch.json` and the loop moves on. If the owner-footage check fails, `$preLaunchViewsIntact` is false and **no** pre-launch runs at all, silently. The measured run goes ahead either way. The leg's only acceptance is on the measured (L4) run: `rows > 0`, `gpuFramesTotal > 0`, `cpuFrames == 0` (`legs/m16-1243-gpu-self-heal-l1-l4.json:14-17`), and `Invoke-VenueLeg.ps1` only copies `self-heal/` without evaluating it (`:561-567`). A run where neither injected 702 tripped the breaker, or L3 never started on CPU, still returns PASS. Receipts from this leg would not satisfy the venue-proof blocker below. Fix: fail the leg unless every pre-launch finished rc 0; L1 and L2 each logged the injected 702 and a breaker trip; the INI after L2 carries the safe-start flag; L3 ran with CUDA suppressed and `cpuFrames > 0`; and L4 shows the flag cleared and CUDA recovered.
- **SHARED BLOCKER (Dot 0b34df09, same tip; CoS-confirmed in code): a fault delivered at AMaZE entry does not stop the call that delivered it.** `platform/qt/GpuDebayer.cpp:957` checks `llrpGpuCudaSuppressed()` once at entry. At `:987`, `enterAmazeStageTelemetry` drains pending render-channel codes as orphans (`:554-560`) through `amazeStageSink` → `stageSink` → `recordFault` (`GpuFieldTelemetry.cpp:472-503,507,536`), which calls the breaker listener synchronously. `GpuDeviceLossBreaker.cpp:164-169` `onFault` sets `llrpSetGpuCudaSuppressed(1)` on that thread. `:988` then calls `runtime.run` anyway. The same order repeats at `:1071`, `:1185` and `:1263`. That breaks the PR's own contract ("after a device loss no CUDA call is made on any thread"). Fix: re-check suppression after `enterAmazeStageTelemetry` and before every backend call.
- **SHARED BLOCKER (Dot 0b34df09, same tip; CoS-confirmed in code): suppression is checked only before the lock that serializes backend work.** `src/mlv/llrawproc/llrawproc.c:2327` checks the flag before `pthread_mutex_lock(&g_llrawproc_gpu_recon_backend_mutex)` at `:2370`. A caller queued on that mutex while the holder loses the device then enters backend work after suppression is set. CoS found the same check-then-lock shape in preupload (`:2169` check, `:2183` trylock), in the GL interop copy (`:3151` check, `:3158` lock), and in the live AMaZE wrappers (`GpuDebayer.cpp:1160`/`:1233` check, `live.mutex` at `:1179`/`:1252`). Fix: re-check under each lock before touching the backend, and keep the entry check as a fast path.
