## Findings

### ADV371-F01 — SHOULD: retain the distinction between fault coverage and diagnosis

Anchor: https://github.com/layibabalola/AdversarialLLM-ClaudeCode/blob/a7a13dc6ea64aa1536411472272f9675d720691d/factory/queue.jsonl#L255

The amended diagnostic row allows outcome A when an injected fault reproduces the recorded failure signature. Empty stdout or RETAIN owner_not_proven_absent can be forced by a chosen stub regardless of whether that mechanism caused the historical intermittent failure. The same-fault fail-before proof establishes the effectiveness of the fixture change for that synthetic condition, but not historical causation. Since this row owns the otherwise-unmeasured remainders and can become DELIVERED, this wording can turn a matching symptom into an overbroad diagnosis/fix claim.

Remedy: explicitly label synthetic fault coverage and any fixture-only mitigation as such. Retain the historical cause as unmeasured unless independent measurement links it to that mechanism. Permit completion of bounded diagnostic work without asserting the historical incident is explained. Do not reintroduce induced CPU load to address this finding.

Regression/evidence plan: a delivery presenting only a timeout/early-exit stub and a matching empty-stdout symptom should not be described as proving the original incident's cause. It should state what the fault proves, what remains unknown, and where any continuing cause investigation resides. ACK or DISTINGUISH is sufficient advisory disposition; this adds no CoS merge gate.

### ADV371-F02 — SHOULD: narrow the claimed equivalence of reduced budgets and saturation

Anchors: https://github.com/layibabalola/AdversarialLLM-ClaudeCode/blob/a7a13dc6ea64aa1536411472272f9675d720691d/factory/queue.jsonl#L235 and https://github.com/layibabalola/AdversarialLLM-ClaudeCode/blob/a7a13dc6ea64aa1536411472272f9675d720691d/factory/queue.jsonl#L256

The new language says what a saturated host would expose is proven by budgets that can fail, and that the behavior saturation exposed is proven by R2's per-call-budget test. Lowering a timeout or sleeping a child can prove budget wiring, bounded error reporting and cleanup. It does not establish the absence of load-dependent scheduling, race, lock or pipe-lifetime failures, nor explain the unknown node.exe holder. The retained unknown-holder language is good, but the blanket equivalence overstates the replacement evidence.

Remedy: describe these as deterministic timeout/fault-path coverage plus host-as-is repeat evidence, explicitly leaving saturation-specific behavior unmeasured. Preserve the no-induced-load requirement and the existing actual lock/cleanup/late-stdout regressions. No extra stress runs are requested.

Regression/evidence plan: the future budget test should establish its named timeout, error details and child cleanup; its report must not claim general saturated-host robustness from that alone. ACK or DISTINGUISH is advisory, not a merge gate.
