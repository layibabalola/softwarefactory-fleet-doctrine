Its finally calls Close-AndroidCaptured without joining the two original drain Tasks.

The new [bounded reader](https://github.com/layibabalola/context-ultra-salesforce/blob/572c4e4013f6afe8041d30f7dc5ed8fa654376d5/tools/android-private/BoundedOutput.cs) deliberately reports an initiating failure before the drain finishes. [Complete at 145–165](https://github.com/layibabalola/context-ultra-salesforce/blob/572c4e4013f6afe8041d30f7dc5ed8fa654376d5/tools/android-private/process.ps1#L145-L165) short-circuits at that failure. Root WaitForExit is not an asynchronous-pipe join. A legal interleaving therefore records 65 bytes against the 64-byte cap, reports overflow, and reaches the fixture's EOF assertion before a reader continuation observes EOF. The other stream can also remain pending. [Close at 131–138](https://github.com/layibabalola/context-ultra-salesforce/blob/572c4e4013f6afe8041d30f7dc5ed8fa654376d5/tools/android-private/process.ps1#L131-L138) correctly refuses unresolved readers, but this finally can mask the initial assertion. Separate [controlled late-EOF tests at 243–275](https://github.com/layibabalola/context-ultra-salesforce/blob/572c4e4013f6afe8041d30f7dc5ed8fa654376d5/tools/tests/android-captured-output.tests.ps1#L243-L275) explicitly demonstrate the pending state and join after releasing EOF; they do not repair this native helper.

This is a source-confirmed scheduling race, not an executed reproduction or attribution to full82/current CI. Remedy: retain the initiating failure, boundedly join each original drain before terminal assertions or close, treat expected faulted completion separately from accepted output, and retain unresolved originals/first error if a join fails. Do not roll back early failure notification. Proposed **NativeByteLimit_EarlyOverflow_DelayedDrainJoin** should independently delay stdout/stderr after root exit and verify original error identity, later EOF, no success promotion and no cleanup masking.
