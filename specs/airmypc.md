## Provider continuity — ratified locally 2026-08-09 22:3x CT

AirMyPC adopts fleet `FAILOVER.md` and its **Provider-neutral amendment** by citation. Providers are
replaceable inference runners; roles, claims, leases, adjudication, and release authority stay local.
Capacity loss attaches to a credential/quota domain as `QUOTA-DORMANT(reset_eta)`, not to a seat as
death. One exhausted account is not a provider outage.

The content gate remains two-key and fail-closed, but a key is provider-neutral. An admitted verifier
must be a registered, drill-qualified actor that did not author or implement the subject and is from
a different qualified provider/`independence_class` than the implementer. Same-provider implementation
and review never form two independent keys. Missing or expired evidence is `UNEVALUABLE`; work banks
or queues rather than releasing.

The provider registry separates seven axes: provider, credential/quota domain, model, CLI transport,
lane role, authority, and subject assignment. Two accounts or wrappers over one inference backend are
one independence class until proven otherwise.

## Health evidence

Absence-based and narrative-transcript regex classifiers are retired for capacity routing. Health
comes only from structured process state plus bounded refusal evidence:

- `READY` / `HEALTHY`: positive completed-sitting or capability receipt within its expiry.
- `QUOTA-DORMANT(reset_eta)`: direct quota refusal with a parsed reset ETA.
- `TRANSIENT`: structured retryable process/API result.
- `AUTH-REQUIRED`: human-only auth/account action; never capacity failover.
- `DOWN`: terminal structured failure within its measurement window.
- `UNEVALUABLE`: missing, stale, ambiguous, or unknown-shape evidence; route nothing new.

Every wake writes a receipt, including stand-down. `IGNITED`, launcher exit alone, auth alignment,
lane-file silence, and prose output are not delivery/capacity evidence. The local ignition floor still
owes a final-child outcome mechanism before it can be detector-of-record.

## Laws and traps exported

- A provider is a runner, never a seat identity or authority.
- Exhaustion is a quota-domain routing event with reset ETA, not seat death.
- Two keys must be distinct qualified inference families; same-family review is not independence.
- Never infer domain independence from separate launch paths/config directories.
- Never classify health by regexing agent narrative; structured exit + bounded STDERR only.
- Timeout with useful stdout but no terminal receipt is `UNEVALUABLE`, not PASS and not empty failure.
- A provider portal is seatless observation only and cannot supply liveness, provenance, or a gate key.
