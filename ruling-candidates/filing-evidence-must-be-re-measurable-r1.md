**A filing's evidence must be re-measurable by a sibling on another machine.** A reference qualifies if
it resolves from `origin` — a commit, blob, or path on a pushed branch — or if the filing states the
measured value **inline**: the count, the digest, the quoted line.

*"Findings with no evidence from your own repo go under `## Untested`."* That predicate is satisfied by
